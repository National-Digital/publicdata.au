# Governance

## Who decides

publicdata.au is run by **National Digital**. National Digital maintains the project, reviews
contributions and is responsible for what is published.

No government agency runs, funds or endorses publicdata.au, and no publisher has endorsed the
copies of its data served here. Decisions are made by the maintainers in the public interest of
keeping Australian open government data usable and available.

## The rules that decide the code

The project's commitments are enforced by the build rather than promised.
[`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#rules-that-decide-the-code) sets them out, the
archive's included, and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) describes how a change is made.

## Contributing and conduct

See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how changes are proposed and checked, and
[`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for how we expect people to behave.
