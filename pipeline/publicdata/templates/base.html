publicdataau

Built and operated by National Digital, Burleigh Heads, Queensland. This is a private website. No government agency runs or has endorsed it.

Data is republished under each publisher's licence, usually CC BY 3.0 AU or CC BY 4.0. The attribution string is inside every file.

Wrong value? It is almost always wrong in the source as well, because we publish what the publisher publishes. Wrong serialisation? Tell us and we will fix it and record the fix.
