# The publicdata.au name and mark

The code is free to reuse under the AGPL. The name and the identity are not, so that people can
always tell the genuine service from a fork or a mirror.

**Legal basis.** publicdata.au does not rely on a registered trade mark. The name and identity are
protected by copyright in the original brand assets, by the tort of passing off, and by the
misleading or deceptive conduct provisions of the Australian Consumer Law (Competition and Consumer
Act 2010 (Cth), Schedule 2). Nothing here claims a registration.

## What is protected

- The name **publicdata.au**, and names close enough to be mistaken for it.
- The mark: the navy tile with an orange dot drawn by `pipeline/publicdata/brand.py`, and the
  social cards built around it.
- The statement that the site is run by **National Digital**, and National Digital's own name and
  identity.

## Typography

Site copy, and the text copied to the data hubs, uses no em-dashes. The gate fails a page that has
one, leaving out text quoted from the publisher, and the hub tests check the hub text.

## Running your own copy

You may run a copy of this code under its licence. If you do, it must not look like publicdata.au
or say it is run by National Digital:

- Change the site name and address (`SITE` and the names in `pipeline/publicdata/brand.py` and
  `site.py`), the mark and the colours.
- Replace the operator, contact, security and terms details, which name National Digital.
- Replace every verification and registration value in `site.py`, such as the analytics token, the
  origin trial token, the MCP Registry key and the directory claims. They belong to this site.
- Keep each dataset's attribution to its publisher, and say that the publisher has not endorsed
  your copy, as this site does.

## Saying you use publicdata.au

You may say that your work uses data or code from publicdata.au, and link to it, as long as you do
not suggest that National Digital or any publisher endorses your work. To cite a dataset, use the
citation on its page.
